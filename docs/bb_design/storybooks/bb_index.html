How backback
is built.

backback is a small, fast app for writing C# on a Mac. It is written in Rust. It lets other free tools do the hard work.

Read the pages in order. First you see the big parts. Then you see the small parts inside them. At the end you see what runs on your Mac, and the rules that keep it safe.
